# MBA Casework: Finance and Strategy

Shorter cases and assignments from the PGDM at Great Lakes Institute of Management, Chennai. Each folder leads with the decision the case asked for and how the analysis answered it.

## Finance

| Case | The question | The answer | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Lufthansa FX hedging](finance/lufthansa-fx-hedging/) | Was Lufthansa's 1985 half-hedge of a US$500 million Boeing order a firing offence? | No. The process was sound and the loss came from an unforecastable currency move. The lesson is instrument choice: options or a financed collar where flexibility matters, the money-market hedge for certainty | Report, hedging model |
| [Option strategy design](finance/option-strategy-design/) | How should an investor protect a US$400,000 stock position he must sell in January? | Compared covered calls, protective puts and a zero-cost collar. The collar locks a US$350,000 floor while keeping upside towards his target | Strategy model |
| [Paisa Vasool business case](finance/paisa-vasool-business-case/) | Should a 450-branch lender fund a digital lending transformation? | Proceed. NPV of +₹378.6 crore at a 12% WACC, 44% IRR and a 3.9-year discounted payback, and the case does not hinge on one assumption | Business case, benefit-cost model |
| [Anandam credit decision](finance/anandam-credit-decision/) | Should the bank lend ₹50 million to a fast-growing garment maker? | Decline the request and offer a smaller facility. The ask is 5.5 times the balance sheet, and return on equity holds up only because of borrowing | Lending deck, credit analysis |
| [ABC liquidity stress test](finance/abc-liquidity-stress-test/) | Should ABC Manufacturing get a further ₹500 crore working capital line? | Approve with conditions. Five stress scenarios show thin headroom, so the approval comes with quick ratio and interest cover covenants and restricted use of proceeds | Stress-test model |

| [Tata Steel digital transformation](strategy/tata-steel-digital-transformation/) | How far should Tata Steel's Aashiyana platform go beyond selling steel? | Earn the platform in stages. Open first to Tata Group products, then to outside partners only on proof, and keep distributors as the delivery backbone because they are the moat | Case deck |
